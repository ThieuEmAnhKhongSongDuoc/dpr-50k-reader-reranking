\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Interpretation}
Official regex matching raises CuratedTREC corpus coverage to 87.75\%; the previous low-coverage explanation is withdrawn. The gap between coverage and Hit@20 shows that answer availability alone does not ensure high ranking accuracy. Reader rescoring improves CuratedTREC Hit@20 over DPR Multi but cannot add passages outside its candidate set.

The cross-encoder yields smaller Hit@20 gains over DPR than the Reader on all evaluated splits, while Hit@100 stays fixed because both models reorder the same $K=100$ candidates. TriviaQA span Exact Match is lower than passage Hit@100: finding evidence and extracting an exact answer are different outcomes. These are inference comparisons, not evidence of statistical significance or new model training.

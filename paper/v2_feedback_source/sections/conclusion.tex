\section{Conclusion}
The corrected evaluation changes the interpretation of CuratedTREC: DPR regex matching finds answer strings in 609 of 694 questions in the 50,000-passage corpus, rather than the 217 found by literal-string matching. The corrected full-test baseline table and ID-normalized BM25+DPR fusion now cover all five benchmarks. The local fusion is slightly above DPR Multi at Hit@20 and Hit@100 on each set under the recorded raw-score combination. Reader conclusions remain pending the required FP16 CuratedTREC and TriviaQA reruns, including development-based candidate selection and TriviaQA span Exact Match. The paper will report those results before submission; no conclusion about Reader gains is drawn from the superseded TREC matcher or incomplete TriviaQA run.

\section{Conclusion}
The corrected evaluation changes the interpretation of CuratedTREC: DPR regex matching finds an answer match for 609 of 694 questions in the 50,000-passage corpus, rather than the 217 counted by the former literal-string check. The corrected BM25, DPR, Reader, and BM25+DPR results use the official CuratedTREC regex evaluator. Reader reranking improves Hit@20 by 4.47 percentage points on CuratedTREC and 7.75 points on TriviaQA relative to DPR Multi; the unchanged candidate set limits gains at Hit@100. TriviaQA Reader span Exact Match is 12.54\% on test. The ID-normalized BM25+DPR fusion is slightly above DPR Multi at Hit@20 and Hit@100 on each benchmark under the recorded raw-score combination. These findings describe inference on a 50,000-passage sample with released checkpoints, not a reproduction of DPR training or its full Wikipedia-corpus evaluation.

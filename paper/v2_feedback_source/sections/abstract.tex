\begin{abstract}
We study Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR) inference on a fixed reservoir sample of 50,000 Wikipedia passages. Full-test lexical, dense, and hybrid references cover five QA benchmarks and 28,219 questions. We correct CuratedTREC answer matching to use the official DPR regular-expression evaluator; the resulting answer-string coverage is 87.75\% for CuratedTREC and 65.68\% overall. After passage-ID normalization, a local BM25+DPR raw-score fusion slightly exceeds DPR Multi Hit@20 and Hit@100 on all five test sets. We also prepare a feedback evaluation of the official extractive Reader on CuratedTREC and TriviaQA, with its candidate budget selected on TriviaQA development data and span Exact Match computed from the Reader head. The corrected FP16 Reader measurements are pending the Kaggle run and are intentionally omitted from this draft abstract. These results concern inference on a reduced corpus and are not directly comparable to the original 21-million-passage benchmark.
\end{abstract}

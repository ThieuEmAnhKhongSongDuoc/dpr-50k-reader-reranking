\subsection{Reader Reranking and Span Exact Match}
The official DPR Reader was run at $K=100$ in FP16 on the full CuratedTREC and TriviaQA test sets. TriviaQA development was run first at the same budget. It reorders each saved DPR top-100 candidate list using its relevance head. Reader span Exact Match is reported separately for TriviaQA because answer-containing-passage Hit@k does not require an exact answer span.

On TriviaQA development, Reader span Exact Match is 12.50\% (1,105/8,837); on the held-out test set it is 12.54\% (1,419/11,313). Retrieving an answer-bearing passage does not guarantee that the Reader predicts the exact answer span.

\subsection{Cross-Encoder Comparison}
We scored the same saved 100 candidate IDs with the frozen MS MARCO cross-encoder in FP32. Table~\ref{tab:reranking} compares the original DPR ordering, cross-encoder ordering, and Reader ordering using the same candidate IDs and answer-hit labels; CuratedTREC uses DPR's official regex matching. Relative to DPR order, cross-encoder Hit@20 increases by 1.59 percentage points on CuratedTREC, 0.34 points on TriviaQA development, and 0.27 points on TriviaQA test. The Reader remains higher at Hit@20 on all three splits. Hit@100 is identical across rankings because each method only reorders the same candidates. The table note records a small discrepancy between the paired candidate-level TriviaQA DPR counts and the independent first-stage aggregate; the paired comparison uses the saved candidate records consistently. Per-query files retain all candidate IDs, DPR ranks, Reader relevance scores, answer-hit labels, cross-encoder logits, and cross-encoder ranks for audit and paired analysis.

\subsection{Corrected CuratedTREC Evaluation and Corpus Coverage}
Using DPR's official regular-expression matching changes CuratedTREC corpus coverage from the previous literal-string count of 217 to 609 of 694 questions (87.75\%). The earlier 31.27\% coverage estimate and the interpretation based on it are withdrawn. Across all five tests, 18,534 of 28,219 questions have an answer match somewhere in the 50,000-passage corpus (65.68\%).

\input{tables/datasets}

On the full CuratedTREC test, corrected Hit@20/Hit@100 is 40.92\%/55.48\% for BM25, 43.52\%/63.83\% for the NQ-Single transfer checkpoint, 51.15\%/68.01\% for DPR Multi, 55.62\%/68.01\% after Reader reranking, and 52.16\%/68.16\% for BM25+DPR. Reader reranking therefore improves the top-20 answer-hit rate by 4.47 percentage points over DPR Multi, while the top-100 hit count stays fixed at 472/694 because the candidate set is unchanged. Among the 609 covered questions, DPR Multi retrieves a matching answer in its top 20 for 58.29\%; BM25+DPR reaches 59.44\%. At top 100, the corresponding conditional values are 77.50\% and 77.67\%. These conditional rates use only covered questions and are reported alongside, not instead of, full-test accuracy.

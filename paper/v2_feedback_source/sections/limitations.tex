\section{Limitations}
The evaluation uses one fixed reservoir sample of 50,000 passages from the 21-million-passage Wikipedia corpus. Sampling changes both answer availability and the distribution of distractors. Measured coverage is an answer-string or regex availability diagnostic, not a semantic relevance judgment; a matching expression can occur in a passage that does not support the answer.

All DPR and Reader weights are released author checkpoints. The experiments evaluate inference and do not reproduce DPR training or update model weights. The corrected BM25+DPR baseline uses a raw weighted score sum; its values depend on the recorded component scores and coefficient.

The study uses one corpus seed and one run per configuration, so it does not estimate variation across corpus samples or establish statistical significance. IndexFlatIP gives exact inner-product results for the supplied vectors, but this revision does not measure an HNSW--FlatIP speed/recall tradeoff.

The Reader is evaluated on a finite $K=100$ candidate set produced by DPR and cannot recover answer-bearing passages outside it. Span predictions are affected by the 350-token input limit. Reader Exact Match is meaningful for literal answer strings; CuratedTREC is evaluated with regex-based answer-hit matching rather than span Exact Match. These are retrieval and reranking measurements, not a complete open-domain QA evaluation.
